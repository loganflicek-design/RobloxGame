# Build a retention-first, odds-free creature game

**Recommendation: build an original cozy-chaos creature-tending game, working title "Moonlit Menagerie", that copies the structure of the current winners (an idle-income collection loop, a social co-op moment every few minutes, and a live-ops calendar) while avoiding the three things that currently carry risk: named brainrot characters, paid random items, and short-term monetization pressure.** As of late October 2026 the charts are led by Murder Mystery 2 and Steal An Egg, followed by Brookhaven RP, 99 Nights in the Forest, Steal a Brainrot, Adopt Me!, Jujutsu Shenanigans, RIVALS and Blox Fruits, though the rank order and every concurrent-user figure come from third-party trackers that disagree with one another ([Endgame Tools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts), via search summary). The pattern worth copying is a one-sentence loop, a social or trading layer, and constant events. The pattern worth fearing is the brainrot decay curve: one tracker reports Steal a Brainrot fell about 99% from its 25.8M peak ([Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)). Roblox's June 2026 shift to a 28-day retention view of discovery now rewards sticky games over monetization-heavy ones ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)). Most figures in this report are unverified because the underlying research could not fetch full pages (details in the confidence section), so treat numbers as directional and use the open-questions list to close gaps before committing money or marketing. The MVP below is deliberately small enough for a beginner plus Claude to ship in a handful of focused sessions.

## The chart is a rotation of seasonal breakouts on top of durable evergreens

The top of the October 2026 chart has two layers. The durable layer is Murder Mystery 2, Brookhaven RP, Adopt Me! and Blox Fruits, which sit in the top 10 month after month. The breakout layer is Steal An Egg (released July 25, 2026), Grow a Garden 2 (June 12, 2026) and the horror title Animal Hospital (May 10, 2026). Steal An Egg is the dominant new hit: one monthly roundup puts it at about 1.6M concurrent players on Sept 30, roughly five times second-place Brookhaven RP at 329.6K, and up 468% on its August figure ([StudioKrew Sept 2026](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-september-2026/)). A second tracker independently shows it second on the Oct 25 chart at 1.6M ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/roblox-player-count)). Grow a Garden 2 reached over 400K concurrent within 20 minutes of launch ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78614-grow-a-garden-2-has-launched-on-roblox-here-s-everything-new-added-in-this-sequel)), and Animal Hospital regularly holds about 350K with reported peaks above 700K ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78741-animal-hospital-becomes-roblox-s-latest-horror-sensation-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know)).

Murder Mystery 2 shows how unreliable the numbers are. It set an all-time record of 1,349,555 concurrent players around Aug 16, 2026 during a summer event ([StudioKrew July 2026](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-july-2026/)), yet the October chart readings of 1.54M and 2.2M come from the same page and contradict each other, while another tracker page showed only 433K online with a 30-day change of -37.7% ([RoWatcher MM2](https://rowatcher.com/games/66654135/murder-mystery-2)). We cannot tell whether this is stale snapshots, measurement differences, or bot filtering. The practical conclusion is to trust ordering within a tracker more than any absolute number, and to treat claims such as Steal An Egg's 14.2M peak as suspect, since an almost identical figure is attributed to 99 Nights in the Forest elsewhere ([OtakuKart](https://otakukart.com/robloxs-steal-an-egg-reaches-6-billion-visits-and-14-2-million-peak-concurrent-players/)).

What the winners share is easier to establish than their exact size. Their loops can be said in one sentence: buy or steal rarer creatures from a conveyor and defend a base (Steal a Brainrot, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)); rank pets by money-per-second (Steal An Egg, per [Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/steal-an-egg-pet-value-list)); plant, grow, collect pets and return tomorrow, in short mobile sessions (Grow a Garden, per [Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-fastest-growing-roblox-games-in-march-2026)); hundreds of tradeable items whose seasonal boxes revalue old inventories (MM2, per StudioKrew above). Each adds a social layer (stealing, trading or co-op) and an event cadence. Grow a Garden's Mega Harvest drew 7M+ RSVPs ([AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/grow-a-garden-events-schedule/)), and 99 Nights ships seasonal eggs, cosmetics and a beekeeping event ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/99-nights-in-the-forest-events-schedule/)).

Two cautions about cadence. Grow a Garden's developer said weekly events were hard to keep fresh and moved toward depth instead ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/roblox-grow-a-garden-major-changes-coming-update-cooking-3884432)), so event fatigue is real for a small team. And the platform is broadening: the top 10 games took about 20% of hours in Q2 2026 versus about 30% three years earlier ([RBLX 2Q26 summary](https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/summaries/RBLX-2Q26-AI-Summary)), which is the most encouraging fact for a newcomer. Our reading of virality, that creator amplification plus a loop a spectator understands in seconds drives launches, is an inference. The one concrete signal is that Steal An Egg was promoted through the Misfits Gaming network ([Misfits on X](https://x.com/MisfitsGG/status/2090785862632038706)); no developer postmortem was found for any 2026 hit.

## Brainrot is past peak, and the 2026 discovery change punishes pressure-selling

The meme layer of Roblox moves in quarters. The documented arc is Grow a Garden peaking at about 22.3M in August 2025, Steal a Brainrot passing 25M in October 2025, then collapse: Brainrot at roughly 215K concurrent by the time of the Rowatcher analysis, with a reported ~80% drop within two weeks of its peak, while Grow a Garden held its audience for months ([Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals); [RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/insights/grow-a-garden-ccu-record-analysis)). Peak figures differ by source, so the shape (fast rise, steep decay, evergreen collectible loops outlasting meme loops) is more reliable than the digits. Brainrot-style games still led genre concurrency in early 2026 at about 1.4M across 29 tracked titles ([Creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/trending-roblox-game-genres-2026), aggregator), but we expect that sub-genre to keep shrinking; this is our inference, not a sourced forecast.

The legal picture reinforces avoiding named meme characters. Steal a Brainrot's owners are in federal litigation in the Northern District of California with Mementum Lab, which claims rights to characters including Tung Tung Sahur; the dispute is undecided and sources differ on party names and dates ([Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2527463/ai-brainrot-fight-tests-copyright-tm-reach-of-viral-memes); [Plagiarism Today, Oct 7 2026](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2026/10/07/who-owns-this-brainrot-character/)). Original characters in a brainrot-flavored spirit, generic mechanics, and generic slang are the safe zone; the licensed-anime games on the charts depend on licences we do not have. Gen Alpha slang decays fast and some terms turn cringe quickly ([Euronews DE](https://de.euronews.com/2026/09/28/von-chud-bis-bop-meistgesuchte-gen-alpha-slangworter-2026-bisher)), so slang should be flavor text that can be swapped without a rebuild. Seasonal hooks exist but are short: Roblox's Halloween event "The Hollow" was reported to start Oct 22, 2026 for about two weeks ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/the-hollow-roblox-halloween-event-2026-games-list/)), so any build started now will miss it.

On the platform side, the most decision-relevant facts are about discovery and policy. Recommended For You selects candidates per user, then ranks on engagement, retention, monetization, friend plays, similarity and variety, and tests new games on small audiences first ([Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery), via search summary). In June 2026 Roblox moved from a 7-day to a 28-day retention view, and on the Q2 call management said impressions fell for games emphasizing short-term monetization while stickier games gained ([Q2 2026 transcript](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/32789865_2009724400_3775763_Transcript_EditedCopy_20260730233208.pdf)). Misleading titles and thumbnails are explicitly deprioritized ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/03/20/roblox-discovery-roadmap-2025/)). The compliance items that shape design are these. Every experience needs a content maturity label, and unlabeled games are restricted ([Content maturity docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/content-maturity)). Chat requires an age check worldwide since January 2026, with chat limited to adjacent age bands ([Business Wire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260107986568/en/Roblox-Requires-Users-Worldwide-to-Age-Check-to-Access-Chat)). Paid random items (eggs, wheels, chests, luck boosts, pity systems) must show every outcome with numeric odds summing to 100%, behind a descriptively labeled pop-up if the list is long ([Paid random items docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)), and users under 18 in Brazil cannot access them at all unless the creator uses PolicyService ([Roblox Brazil update](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/cs/articles/48706630819476-Brazil-Digital-ECA-Updates)).

Monetization economics are modest and the platform backdrop is soft. Creators keep 70% of game passes, developer products and private servers ([obby.fun](https://www.obby.fun/blog/how-to-make-money-on-roblox), secondary). Creator Rewards, which replaced engagement-based payouts in July 2025, pay 5 Robux per Active Spender who plays 10+ minutes in a day ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628)), an incentive that again favors repeat daily play. Roblox Plus ($4.99/month) pays creators 250 Robux per month for three months per newly acquired subscriber, with a 60-day hold ([Roblox Plus docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/roblox-plus)). Q2 2026 bookings grew only 8% against 36% revenue growth, which the company attributed to lower per-hour monetization among younger North American users ([Roblox Q2 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm), via search summary). DevEx was reported at $0.0038 per earned Robux from Sept 5, 2025, from third-party calculators only ([bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/tools/roblox-devex-calculator)); on that unverified rate, one Robux of player spend yields about 0.7 earned Robux, or roughly $0.0027 (our arithmetic). Plan for small cash returns and treat this as a craft-and-learning project first.

## The concept: Moonlit Menagerie

This is our recommendation, built by inference from the sources above; none of its parts has been tested. **Pitch: you run a night-time creature sanctuary. Cute original gremlins live in your pens, earn coins while you are away, and every few minutes the whole server drops what it is doing to chase down a mischievous bandit before it steals the sanctuary's treasure.** It borrows the idle-income pet loop (Grow a Garden, Steal An Egg), the conveyor-with-visible-items purchase flow (Steal a Brainrot), and the co-op spectacle that made horror co-op games such as Animal Hospital and 99 Nights spread, while replacing player-versus-player stealing with an NPC thief. That choice avoids griefing on public servers, a concern parent guides raise about brainrot games ([Kinzoo](https://www.kinzoo.com/blog/what-are-brainrot-games-a-parents-guide-to-the-roblox-trend)), and means the social moment works even with chat gated by age checks. The fresh twist is the "Moonrise Chase": a server-wide, timer-driven co-op event that gives clip-able moments for creators, which is our hypothesis about what makes a game spread, not something the research confirmed.

**Core loop (about 2 minutes per cycle).** Walk to the Egg Belt and buy an egg whose contents are shown on the belt before purchase (no randomness, so no loot-box disclosure is required for it). Place it in a pen, where it hatches on a short timer into a gremlin that earns coins per second even while you are offline. Collect coins, buy higher-tier eggs or pen upgrades, and merge three identical gremlins into a higher-tier one. On a shared server clock, Moonrise begins; the bandit runs a looping path, and players tag it with free tools to recover bonus coins and a chance at a limited-time cosmetic gremlin skin from the event pool. Rebirth ("New Moon") resets coins for a permanent multiplier and a new pen biome, in the manner of the rebirth mechanic reported for Steal a Brainrot.

**Progression and retention.** Layer the hooks the sources associate with retention: a daily login reward with a visible streak, an index (collection book) that shows missing gremlins as silhouettes, a weekly themed event rotating gremlin skins, and an offline-earnings summary on return. Because Roblox now optimizes for 28-day retention and Creator Rewards pay for 10+ minute daily sessions, the design should reward coming back each day more than long single sessions. Keep the event cadence achievable for a tiny team: one weekly rotation plus one larger monthly update, rather than the weekly new-content treadmill Grow a Garden's developer found unsustainable. We found no sourced evidence on streak or daily-reward effect sizes, so these are plausible design bets measured through Roblox's built-in retention analytics ([Analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics)).

**Monetization (policy-compliant by construction).** Sell game passes for convenience and capacity (2x coins, auto-collect, extra pens), developer products for repeatable coin packs and instant-hatch of a visible, known gremlin, and a paid private-server subscription. At launch, include no paid randomness: no luck boosts, no paid wheels, no gacha eggs. If random rewards come later, free-earned randomness (the Moonrise reward drop) is the lower-risk route; paid randomness requires full percentage odds in a labeled pop-up, a PolicyService check for Brazil, and ideally a guaranteed purchase alternative, per the docs cited above. Skip pressure tactics (countdown-to-lose offers, prompts that interrupt play), since the discovery algorithm now penalizes short-term monetization emphasis and the audience skews young. Rewarded video ads require an average of 100K+ DAU, which is out of reach early ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-creators-can-now-use-rewarded-video-ads/3838678)), and a planned Studio plug-in is reported only by one outlet ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417959/)). Do not rely on the single-source claim that a Roblox Plus subscription is a developer requirement; it was uncorroborated.

**Mobile-first UI plan.** About 80% of Roblox DAU is on mobile ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/80-of-roblox-users-are-on-mobile-contributing-46-of-robux-revenue)), so design for a phone in landscape first. The genre template, inferred from commercial UI kits rather than observed in top games, is a currency and stat readout in the top corners, a persistent button column for Shop, Pens, Index and Rebirth on a screen edge, a capacity bar, and boost timers; kits describe a bright, readable style ([Modern Cartoon Simulator UI](https://builtbybit.com/resources/modern-cartoon-simulator-ui.109159/)). Keep interactive UI inside the default CoreUISafeInsets so notches and the top bar do not clip it, size with scale plus UIAspectRatioConstraint and UISizeConstraint, and use tweens and sounds for coin pops and hatch reveals ([ScreenInsets docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/ScreenInsets.md); [Size modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/ui/size-modifiers)). A common unofficial guideline puts the minimum touch target at 44x44 px ([creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/blog/roblox-ui-design-best-practices)); that is not from Roblox docs, so verify on real phones. For onboarding, follow Roblox's guidance to teach essentials, reach the fun quickly and A/B test steps ([Onboarding docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/game-design/onboarding)): our target is a first hatched gremlin earning coins within the first 60 seconds with no text walls. Use plain Instances plus UIScale and tweens first; adopt Fusion or React-lua only if the shop and inventory lists become painful to maintain.

## Minimal MVP and first-steps checklist

The repo already has the Rojo skeleton (`default.project.json` mapping `src/server`, `src/client`, `src/shared`, with Rojo 7.4.4 pinned in `rokit.toml`) and one stub file in each folder. The MVP is one map, one server-authoritative economy, and the smallest slice that tests the hypothesis "players come back for the pens and the Moonrise Chase".

| MVP scope (ship) | Deferred (do not build yet) |
|---|---|
| One small lobby map with 6 pens per player and one shared Egg Belt | Multiple biomes and rebirth biomes |
| 3 egg tiers, 9 gremlins (3 per tier), coins-per-second income, 3-to-1 merge | Trading, gifting, player-versus-player anything |
| Moonrise Chase every ~5 minutes: one NPC bandit, one tag tool, shared bonus coins | Limited-time cosmetic pool and weekly event rotation |
| Data saving with ProfileStore, offline earnings, daily reward with streak | Index book beyond a simple silhouette grid |
| Mobile-first HUD: coins, shop, pens, daily reward | Game passes beyond 2x coins and extra pens; private servers can follow |
| Maturity questionnaire, honest icon and 16:9 thumbnails | Ads, sponsorship, codes, group perks |

Suggested build order, each step ending in a Studio playtest: (1) run `rokit install` and `rojo serve`, connect Studio, and confirm the existing stub scripts sync; (2) write a server `DataService` using ProfileStore with session locking and save-on-leave, then a stub that grants a starter gremlin ([ProfileStore](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/profilestore/3190543)); (3) define gremlins and eggs as data tables in `src/shared` so content is edited in one place; (4) build the pen-and-income loop on the server, with the client only requesting actions through RemoteEvents that the server validates (server-authoritative design is a standard anti-exploit principle, though no source in our notes covered it); (5) build the HUD and shop with scale-based sizing and test in Studio's phone emulator; (6) add the Egg Belt and hatch reveal juice; (7) add the Moonrise Chase with a server clock; (8) add the daily reward and offline earnings; (9) complete the content maturity questionnaire, set up an honest icon and thumbnails, and publish to a small test group; (10) read the retention analytics before adding anything else, and only then consider a small Ads Manager budget, which Roblox suggests only after onboarding is solid ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)). Add Selene and StyLua and a CI check once the first scripts exist; the toolchain recommendation (Rojo, Rokit, Wally, Selene, StyLua) comes from third-party guides ([oflight](https://www.oflight.co.jp/ja/columns/luau-developer-toolchain-rojo-selene-wally-2026)).

## What we know, what is shaky, and what we still need

The research behind this report could not fetch full pages: the roblox.com hosts and the main trackers were blocked or failed DNS, so almost every claim comes from search-result summaries. That makes the confidence tiers below important.

| Confidence | Items |
|---|---|
| Reasonably supported (primary docs seen through summaries, or consistent across sources) | Paid random item odds rule; Brazil under-18 restriction; maturity-label requirement; age-checked chat since Jan 2026; 70/30 revenue split; Creator Rewards structure; Roblox Plus creator payout terms; discovery's shift to a 28-day retention view; ScreenInsets and size-constraint guidance; Steal An Egg being a top-2 game in late Oct 2026; Steal a Brainrot's steep decay from its 2025 peak |
| Third-party or conflicting | All CCU and visit figures; exact rank order; MM2 current level (1.5M-2.2M vs a 433K reading); Steal An Egg's 14.2M peak and 6B visits; Steal a Brainrot and Grow a Garden peak values and decay percentages; Q2 2026 DAU (123M vs 152M); DevEx rate of $0.0038; the $64M Bloomberg estimate for Steal a Brainrot; quarterly bookings figures |
| Unverified or single-source | Steal An Egg being pulled by Roblox and restored (cause and date unknown); a bot purge distorting MM2 counts; the participating-game list for The Hollow; a cross-experience sale shutdown on May 30, 2026; Kids/Select tiers tied to Roblox Plus; KreekCraft quitting Roblox; any creator subscriber counts |
| Not found at all | Conversion benchmarks; official D1/D7 targets; retention and daily-reward effect sizes; how any 2026 hit went viral; dominant art styles and thumbnail click-through data; actual HUD layouts of top games; low-end mobile performance guidance; anti-exploit postmortems; an October 2026 meme ranking from TikTok or Know Your Meme |

The open questions that matter most before spending heavily are these. First, pull a clean, dated snapshot from Roblox Discover, Rolimons or RoMonitor for places 1-20 with 30- and 90-day trends, so we know which genres are rising rather than which are merely large. Second, play the top five games on a phone and record the first 60 seconds, HUD layout, daily reward flow, shop placement and event cadence ourselves, since no source analyzed this. Third, read the primary Creator Docs for discovery, paid random items, content maturity, DevEx and developer requirements in a browser with working access, and confirm the DevEx rate and the Roblox Plus claim. Fourth, find developer postmortems or RDC talks on Steal An Egg, Grow a Garden 2 and Animal Hospital to test our virality hypothesis, and identify which creators actually move Roblox traffic today. Fifth, check Q3 2026 earnings (expected late October or early November) for DAU and bookings. Sixth, ask whether the Moonrise Chase is fun and clippable: a paper design and a ten-minute playtest with a few real players of the target age should come before any art investment. Seventh, decide the legal hygiene steps: a name and character trademark search for "Moonlit Menagerie" and our gremlin designs, and confirmation that no included asset or slang term is tied to a disputed meme IP.

## Conclusion

The research changes the question from "which game is hottest" to "which shape survives". The evidence says hot meme loops decay in months while collectible, return-tomorrow loops hold audiences, and Roblox has now tuned discovery toward the latter, so a small team's best edge is not copying Steal An Egg's mechanics but being cheap to keep fresh and honest to the algorithm: daily reasons to return, a shared moment that creators can clip, and a store with nothing random in it.

The biggest risk to this plan is not policy but evidence quality. We are betting on a retention thesis assembled from third-party trackers, search snippets and one team's reading of platform changes, and the right response is to ship the narrow MVP quickly, read the first real D1 and D7 numbers, and let those, not this report, decide whether Moonlit Menagerie earns a second month.
