## Which games are top by CCU right now, and which are the fastest-rising breakouts in the last 1-3 months?

### Takeaway
Late-October 2026 charts are led by Murder Mystery 2 and Steal An Egg (the summer 2026 breakout), with Brookhaven RP, 99 Nights in the Forest, Steal a Brainrot, Adopt Me!, Jujutsu Shenanigans, RIVALS and Blox Fruits filling out the top 10. The fastest risers of the last 1-3 months are Steal An Egg (launched July 25, 2026) and Grow a Garden 2 (June 2026), with Animal Hospital (May 2026) as a spring horror breakout. Reported rankings and CCU conflict between sources, so rank order is approximate.

## What genres dominate, and what do the winners share?

### Takeaway
The charts mix collect/steal/trade "brainrot-style" and pet-egg loops (Steal An Egg, Steal a Brainrot, Grow a Garden, Adopt Me!), social deduction (MM2), co-op horror (99 Nights, Animal Hospital), roleplay (Brookhaven), anime/fighting (Blox Fruits, Jujutsu Shenanigans) and competitive shooters (RIVALS). Shared traits are short-loop progression, trading/economy, and frequent events.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot loop: buy rarer Brainrots from a conveyor or steal them from other players' bases (capture-the-flag-like), temporary base shield, rebirth for stat/currency boosts; constant updates add new Brainrots. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot), [findingdulcinea / guides via search](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/steal-a-brainrot-roblox/)
- Steal An Egg: pets ranked by money-per-second (value lists exist), consistent with a steal/idle-income loop. — [Timesaver value list](https://timesaver.gg/blog/steal-an-egg-pet-value-list)
- MM2: social deduction; hundreds of tradeable "godlies", seasonal boxes revalue old inventories, which sustains a trading economy. — [StudioKrew July 2026](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-july-2026/)
- Animal Hospital: observation-based co-op horror, night shifts at a vet clinic, admit only real animals, spot anomalies. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/animal-hospital-on-roblox-how-the-anomaly-horror-game-works/)
- 99 Nights: seasonal/event cadence (egg and cosmetic updates, beekeeping event). — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/99-nights-in-the-forest-events-schedule/)
- Roblox Q2 2026: hours engaged +5% to 29B; top-10 games about 20% of hours vs about 30% three years ago (broader mix). — [RBLX 2Q26 summary](https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/summaries/RBLX-2Q26-AI-Summary)
- Genre overview article. — [ExitLag](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/roblox-game-genres-and-popularity/)

### Inferences
- Winners combine a one-sentence-readable loop, social interaction (stealing, trading, co-op), and a cadence of limited-time events that re-engage lapsed players.

### Gaps
- Session-length data per game not found.
- Retention figures not found; developer update cadence is only known for a few titles.

## What recent breakout hits (2025-2026) were there, and how did they go viral?

### Takeaway
2025 was dominated by Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot and 99 Nights in the Forest (all peaking in summer-fall 2025); 2026 added Grow a Garden 2, Animal Hospital and Steal An Egg. Platform engagement was growing while DAU reporting is being revised for bot mitigation and age checks.

### Cited Findings
- Original Grow a Garden: 21.3M peak per one report; Guinness record claim of 22.3M in Aug 2025 (a different outlet); Steal a Brainrot reportedly passed it in Sept/Oct 2025 at 25M-25.8M (sources disagree on date; one search returned no confirmation of 25M). — [Wikipedia Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden), [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-game-grow-a-garden-beats-steam-with-record-concurrent-user-high-of-89m), [NoPing](https://noping.com/blog/how-many-people-play-roblox). Conflict: the 25M claim is unverified; Wikipedia's Brainrot peak figures refer to a Fortnite island replica.
- Q3 2025 growth driven by Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights; engagement outside top 10 +58% YoY then. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/?p=309290) (via earnings summary)
- Steal An Egg was publicized via Misfits Gaming (a creator/org portfolio), suggesting publisher/creator-network promotion helped launch. — [Misfits on X](https://x.com/MisfitsGG/status/2090785862632038706)
- Roblox DAU: Q2 2026 reported as 123M (+10% YoY) by one source; another source says 152M (likely wrong). Q3 2026 results not found. KPIs subject to bot-mitigation/age-verification refinements. — [Interspace](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/?p=10978), [RBLX 2Q26 summary](https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/summaries/RBLX-2Q26-AI-Summary)
- Best new games list. — [Everything Edinburgh](https://everythingedinburgh.com/games/gaming/best-roblox-games-2026/), [Sportskeeda Oct 2026](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/best-roblox-games-to-play-right-now)

### Inferences
- Viral mechanics seem to be creator/YouTube-TikTok amplification plus a loop spectators can follow instantly (steal, mutate, flip pets), but specific virality evidence for 2026 hits was not found.

- Roblox Q3 2026 earnings (late Oct/early Nov) not yet found.
- Update cadence and retention hooks for most titles not sourced.
