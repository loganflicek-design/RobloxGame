## Which ones are safe to use (trademark, age-appropriate, Roblox policy)?

### Takeaway
Generic mechanics and original characters are safe; named viral AI characters and licensed anime/movie IP carry real legal risk, and Roblox's tightened safety regime affects design (chat, age checks).

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot's owners (Spyder Games, Speedy Simulator Gaming) are in N.D. Cal. litigation with Mementum Lab SAS, which claims rights to brainrot characters incl. Tung Tung Sahur (C&D Sept 2025; trademark/unfair-competition counterclaims; cross motions to dismiss pending; Mementum notes a licensing deal with Epic Games). Not yet decided — [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2527463/ai-brainrot-fight-tests-copyright-tm-reach-of-viral-memes), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/tung-tung-sahur-is-at-the-center-of-a-bizarre-federal-custody-battle-over-brainrot-characters-3381733/), [Plagiarism Today, Oct 7 2026](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2026/10/07/who-owns-this-brainrot-character/), [Haynes Boone](https://www.haynesboone.com/news/publications/bell-and-mason-in-texas-lawyer-ai-slop-or-ip-gold-the-business-of-brainrot). Sources differ on party names and filing dates.
- Roblox requires age checks (facial age estimation or ID) for chat worldwide since Jan 2026; chat off by default under 9 without parental consent; chat limited to adjacent age bands — [Business Wire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260107986568/en/Roblox-Requires-Users-Worldwide-to-Age-Check-to-Access-Chat), [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/roblox-is-updating-chat-rules-with-a-simple-age-check-keeps-you-playing/).
- Roblox faces many lawsuits over child safety; Florida AG subpoena — same Business Wire/GMA coverage ([GMA](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/roblox-announces-new-safety-measure-amid-spate-lawsuits-127626071)).
- Unverified single-source claim: June 2026 "Roblox Kids/Select" tiers and a $4.99 "Roblox Plus" developer requirement — [TechLoy](https://www.techloy.com/roblox-to-launch-kids-accounts-in-major-pivot-following-child-safety-pressure/); not corroborated. Verify before relying on it.
- Parent guides flag brainrot games' monetization pressure and stranger-filled public servers — [Kinzoo](https://www.kinzoo.com/blog/what-are-brainrot-games-a-parents-guide-to-the-roblox-trend).

### Inferences
- Safe: original "brainrot-style" characters you create, generic mechanics (collect, steal, tsunami-run, garden/fishing), Halloween themes, generic slang. Risky: Italian-brainrot named characters (Tung Tung Sahur etc.), anime/movie/brand names without license, Roblox event marks. Make gameplay/monetization kid-appropriate (avoid pressure-selling).

## How did past meme-driven Roblox hits go viral and how fast did they decay?

### Takeaway
Hits spike very fast (weeks) and then decay steeply, with the leader sustaining only through constant updates and event spikes; successors emerge quarterly.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot (Spyder Games/SpyderSammy; launched May 2025) hit record 25.8M CCU in Oct 2025 (some sources 25.4M), then fell to roughly 215K CCU (about -99%) per Rowatcher; Escape Tsunami For Brainrots about 164K; Swing Obby for Brainrots peaked about 87K, now about 57K; Bloomberg-estimated $64M+ spend — [Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals) (via search summary; third-party tracking). Still top-grossing on mobile as of March 2026 — [Rowatcher March 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-fastest-growing-roblox-games-in-march-2026).
- Grow a Garden peaked at 22.3M CCU in Aug 2025, about 20-21B visits — same Rowatcher / [Playnews](https://www.playnews.gg/guides/roblox-les-meilleurs-jeux-du-moment-en-juillet-2026).
- Plants vs Brainrots led Oct 2025 charts — [Games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-top-charts-for-october-2025/).
- Escape Tsunami For Brainrots published Jan 2026 and got a Nintendo Switch release (May 21, 2026) — [Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/store/products/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-switch/); CCU figures conflicting.
- Knockoffs/clones flood search for hits (e.g., "modded" tsunami clones) — Roblox search results above.

### Inferences
- Pattern: meme + dead-simple loop + frequent updates + creator/YouTuber amplification -> 10-25M CCU within ~3-5 months -> 90%+ decay within ~12 months; next hit swaps the reskin (garden -> brainrot -> tsunami/obby). A new game should plan for launch speed, update cadence, and a monetization model that survives a 90% drop.

### Gaps
- No decay figures for Grow a Garden or 99 Nights in the Forest; exact timelines unverified.
